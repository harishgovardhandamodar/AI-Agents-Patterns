**Definition 1 (Draft-only HR agents).** HR satisfies *draft-only agency* iff (i) all HR stores mount read-only to agents, (ii) every hire, fire, pay, and review action requires a named human's recorded approval, (iii) per-sandbox HR credentials scope each agent to its case, and (iv) all reads are logged against named paths with retention for compliance horizons.

**Theorem 1 (Turned agents draft, never decide).** Under draft-only agency, a fully turned HR agent can propose biased screens and draft diverted payroll — but can execute neither: every consequential path terminates at a human who was not turned with it.

*Proof.* Reads are permitted (i) so drafting works; writes and actions require the human (ii), who sees the draft with its reasons attached. A biased screen arrives as a proposal the human reviews against measurable criteria — the bias is visible precisely because the criteria are written. A diverted payroll arrives as a draft the human approves or rejects — the diversion needs the same signature as the legitimate payment. Per-sandbox credentials (iii) keep one turned agent out of other cases' records; logged reads (iv) make every access reconstructible across compliance horizons.
