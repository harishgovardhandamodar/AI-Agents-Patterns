**Definition 1 (Single-bound money movement).** Finance movement satisfies *single binding* iff (i) every transfer waits on human approval with dual control where amounts qualify, (ii) payment credentials are bound once — one sandbox, one rail, revocable in one act — with no standing payment authority outside a gate, and (iii) ledgers are read-only to agents with append-only settlement records plus evidence capture.

**Theorem 1 (Seconds to gate, never past it).** Under single binding, a hijacked objective becomes at most a gated request within seconds — never a completed transfer: injection shapes meet the human gate, threshold-splitting meets per-counterparty reconciliation, retry storms meet idempotency, and voice-deepfake urgency meets the evidence pack.

*Proof.* Injected purchases and fake invoices arrive as requests, not movements — approval plus dual control stands between request and rail. Threshold splitting evades amount tripwires but not per-counterparty reconciliation or the goal detector's objective view. The 14,000-duplicate retry storm meets idempotency (one execution, 13,999 cache hits) and the global retry budget. Urgent bank changes and deepfaked voices meet the evidence pack (rule, history, anomaly flags) rather than an amount and a button — manufactured urgency is exactly what evidence defeats. Ledgers read-only plus append-only settlement mean the record survives whatever the request attempted. Gate one payment path end to end and time the hold; run the guarded finance mix quarterly; verify no standing payment authority exists — the invariant everything else assumes. ∎
