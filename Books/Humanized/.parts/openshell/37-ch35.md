## Chapter 35: Domain playbook: marketing

At stake: Ad spend, campaigns, brand channels, and the analytics that steer them.

Spend and voice are the two authorities; a turned agent spends money and reputation simultaneously. Catalog rehearsal: *Autonomous social agent posts offensive thread from poisoned reply* (1 of the domain's scenarios).

**Definition 1 (Drafts-only voice).** Marketing satisfies *drafts-only voice* iff (i) agents write only to campaign drafts, (ii) every budget change and every public post requires approval, (iii) channel credentials are per-campaign and die with the campaign generation, and (iv) analytics reads are logged per agent with inputs snapshotted before quarantine.

**Theorem 1 (No unapproved spend or speech).** Under drafts-only voice with per-campaign credentials, a turned agent can draft offensive content and propose reckless spend — but can publish neither and spend nothing: both authorities require approvals the agent cannot issue to itself, and the credentials expire with the campaign.

*Proof.* Drafts are consequence-free by construction (writes confined to drafts/). Spend changes need approval (budget at runtime, capped); public posts need approval (voice gated). Per-campaign credentials die with the generation, so authority cannot accumulate across campaigns — each campaign starts unauthorized and earns its scope. Analytics integrity closes the steering loop: logged reads plus pre-quarantine snapshots mean poisoned metrics are attributable to their source. Cap one budget at runtime (not just the dashboard); confirm no standing publish credential exists outside a gate; gate one spend control and one publish path. ∎
