## Chapter 20: Domain playbook: cybersecurity

At stake: Scanner targets, intel feeds, firewall rules, and quarantine exceptions — the guard's own weapons.

A security agent that can both see everything and change the fence is the highest-leverage compromise in the fleet. Catalog rehearsal: *Autonomous pentest agent pivots from authorized scope to production exfil* (1 of the domain's scenarios).

```yaml
version: 1
filesystem_policy:
  read: ["/opt/intel/feeds", "/etc/scanner/targets.d"]
  write: []
process: {user: "agent", capabilities: []}
network_policies:
  - binaries: ["scanner"]
    destinations: ["intel.internal:443"]
    methods: ["GET"]
```

Catalog weight behind this chapter: 2 patterns, 1 scenarios, 3 cascades (inherent 2, emergent 0, derived 1, cascading 0).

**Definition 1 (Gated-guard posture).** The security agent satisfies the *gated-guard posture* if (i) every firewall change, quarantine exception, and scan-target expansion waits on human approval, (ii) scan targets are named narrowly per engagement with expiry, and (iii) intel feed provenance is verified (logged, not assumed) with snapshots before quarantine.

**Theorem 1 (Highest leverage, tightest gate).** Under the gated-guard posture, the highest-leverage compromise in the fleet (a turned security agent) can observe within its scope but cannot change the fence, widen its targets, or suppress findings without a human approving each move.

*Proof.* Observation (scan results, intel reads) flows through read-only mounts and GET-only rules — seeing is permitted, changing is not. Every fence change (firewall, quarantine exception, target expansion) requires human approval (i), so the pivot from authorized scope to production exfil waits at a gate the attacker cannot self-approve. Suppression (hidden findings, widened blast) meets the audit trail plus feed snapshots (iii): what the agent saw versus what was there is reconstructible, and hidden deltas are findings. Apply your own threat model to the security agent first — the guard's weapons deserve the fleet's strictest gates. ∎
