**Definition 1 (Versioned-assumption strategy).** Strategy work satisfies *assumption versioning* iff (i) forecast inputs are versioned and pinned (inputs-v2, never latest), (ii) agents write drafts only, (iii) no strategy output distributes without principal review, and (iv) evidence packs link every claim to logged inputs.

**Theorem 1 (No travelling drafts).** Under assumption versioning with review-before-distribution, a suppressed-dissent council can converge confidently — but its output still waits at the principal's review with dissent records and versioned assumptions attached, and never travels as a decision unreviewed.

*Proof.* Drafts are inert (ii): the confident wrong call exists as a draft, not a decision. Distribution requires principal review (iii), which receives the evidence pack (iv): versioned assumptions, logged inputs, recorded dissent. Suppression fails at the pack: the missing counterargument is visible as a gap in the record the principals explicitly check (write the key assumptions measurably). Forecast gaming meets the pinned inputs (gaming the forecast requires moving inputs that are versioned); intel fabrication meets source logging; plan distortion meets the review. Confirm no strategy output distributes unreviewed: the gate is the only path upward, and everything upward passes through it. ∎
