## Chapter 17: Fail closed: freezes, windows, and the halt

Launch waits for supervisor confirmation and disconnect freezes the agent, so no agent ever runs before its controls are confirmed or unobserved after they vanish. Only the same supervisor process can resume a frozen workload — continuity is unforgeable, and takeover by credential theft fails by design.

The halt is the fleet-scale counterpart: pause everything when doubt is fleet-wide, then attribute at leisure. Confirm the halt path needs no healthy supervisor to work, and rehearse supervisor-kill drills on a schedule — the control you never test is decoration.

**Definition 1 (Fail-closed pair).** The deployment is *fail-closed* iff (i) no workload launches before supervisor confirmation of its boundary, (ii) supervisor disconnect freezes every workload it supervised, (iii) only the same supervisor process resumes a frozen workload, and (iv) the fleet halt executes without requiring any healthy supervisor.

**Theorem 1 (No ungoverned execution).** Under the fail-closed pair, there is no time interval — however brief — during which an agent executes without a confirmed, connected supervisor: pre-launch, mid-run disconnect, and fleet-doubt are all covered, with no degraded mode where protection quietly stops applying.

*Proof.* Pre-launch: (i) gates start on confirmation, so execution begins under governance or not at all. Mid-run disconnect: (ii) freezes on disconnect, so loss of supervision stops execution rather than unobserve it. Resume: (iii) restricts resumption to the same supervisor process, so neither a new supervisor (even with valid credentials) nor a thief can adopt the frozen workload — continuity is unforgeable. Fleet doubt: (iv) halts without needing a healthy supervisor, so the big red option works exactly when supervision is gone. The four clauses partition all supervisor states (confirming, connected, replaced, dead): in each, the agent is either governed or stopped. There is no fifth state — which is precisely what "fail closed" means, and precisely what graceful-degradation instincts (carry over from stateless services) would break. Stateful agents must stop, not limp on: rehearse the outage and confirm the freeze rather than assuming it. ∎
