**Definition 1 (Queue-scoped support).** Support satisfies *queue scoping* iff (i) agents resolve freely within policy but write notes only (never account state), (ii) refunds and access changes wait for humans, (iii) credentials scope per queue and die with the case, and (iv) ticket histories are append-only with evidence capture.

**Theorem 1 (Volume without blast radius).** Under queue scoping, a macro running at volume can resolve a thousand tickets — but can refund none, access nothing outside its queue, and rewrite no history: helpfulness scales, authority does not.

*Proof.* Notes-only writes (i) keep resolution in the commentary layer: the agent's volume applies to words, never to state. Money and access wait for humans (ii), so the refund-farming escalation terminates at a queued request with the full transcript attached — the pattern visible precisely because histories are append-only (iv). Per-queue credentials (iii) bound macros to their queue: volume within bounds, never across them. The card-number shape meets the vault discipline (never see what must not repeat); macro poisoning meets pre-wide-use review. Scope one credential to its queue; gate one refund threshold; confirm histories append-only — volume stays an asset because authority stays gated. ∎
