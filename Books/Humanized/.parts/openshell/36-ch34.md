At stake: Routing decisions, fleet movements, warehouse operations, and customs documents.

**Definition 1 (Proposal-only routing).** Routing satisfies *proposal-only agency* iff (i) agents write only to proposals (never to committed routes, manifests, or customs documents), (ii) every route-plan commit waits at the human gate, (iii) carrier credentials stop at their lanes, and (iv) manifests are append-only with evidence capture.

**Theorem 1 (Rerouting waits at the gate).** Under proposal-only routing, a turned agent can propose any rerouting — but no shipment moves, no manifest changes, and no customs document issues without the human approving the specific proposal.

*Proof.* Proposals are drafts in a proposals directory: visible, reviewable, consequence-free. Committing a proposal (manifest change, fleet movement, customs filing) requires the gate, which sees the full evidence (cost delta, lane scope, counterparty). Carrier credentials stop at their lanes, so even an approved proposal moves only its lane's freight. Counterfeit components enter through steered procurement — the adjacent shape — and meet the same gate with vendor history attached. Rehearse the rerouting variant against the guarded preset; narrow one carrier credential to its lanes; confirm manifests append-only. The scare resolves to an attributable path because every executed move was once a reviewed proposal. ∎
