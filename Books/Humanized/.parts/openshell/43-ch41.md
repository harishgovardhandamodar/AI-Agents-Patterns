**Definition 1 (Draft-free, discount-gated sales).** Sales satisfies *bounded empowerment* iff (i) drafting and enrichment are free within per-territory scopes, (ii) discounts past threshold and bulk exports wait for humans, (iii) CRM writes land only in drafts (never direct record mutation), and (iv) per-territory credentials expire with the case.

**Theorem 1 (Forecast the agent cannot cook).** Under bounded empowerment with versioned stage changes, the forecast is a function of logged drafts plus human-approved discounts — no agent action can alter a recorded stage, invent an approval, or move another territory's numbers.

*Proof.* Drafts are proposals (iii): enriching a quote or touching a record stages a change the pipeline log records with actor and reason. Discounts past threshold need humans (ii); bulk exports need humans; cross-tenant reads are refused by per-territory scoping (iv). The forecast aggregates logged stages — versioned, attributed, human-approved where consequential — so cooking it requires forging the log (append-only), the approval (human), or another territory's credential (unheld). Discount abuse gets rehearsed first by margin impact; contact exfiltration meets the tenant boundary; forecast gaming meets the versioned stage log. Cap one discount authority at runtime; audit which agent paths write the pipeline. ∎
