## Chapter 52: Conclusion: capability without ambient authority

Every agent runs sandboxed, every action crosses policy, every credential stays supervisor-side, and every generation ends on schedule. That is the whole program, and it holds even when every detector misses — which is exactly the property you want when the adversary reads your playbook.

**Definition 1 (Capability without ambient authority).** A fleet operates on *capability without ambient authority* iff every agent can act powerfully (files, packages, APIs, credentials, models) while holding nothing ambient: no standing credentials, no unlisted destinations, no unscoped grants, no cross-generation trust — every authority named, bound, expiring, and enforced by the runtime rather than promised by the model.

**Theorem 1 (The program in one bound).** Under capability without ambient authority, the worst case of total detector failure is bounded by policy: every agent runs sandboxed (reach fixed), every action crosses policy (each act decided), every credential stays supervisor-side (nothing to steal), every generation ends on schedule (nothing persists) — and the conjunction holds without any detector firing, ever.

*Proof.* Sandboxing fixes reach: the compromised agent's action set is the policy's, not its own. Policy crossing decides each act: unlisted destinations never open, unscoped tools never run, irreversible steps wait at gates. Supervisor-side credentials mean total agent compromise yields zero authenticating secrets. Generation ends bound persistence: even undetected compromise dies on schedule. Each clause is detector-independent — enforced by runtime, kernel, and cryptography, none consulting a score. Their conjunction is the whole program: capability (the agent can do powerful things) without ambient authority (nothing held, nothing standing, nothing inherited). Pick one grant, one destination, and one generation age to review — small steady revision beats rare overhaul, and the program compounds exactly like the dossiers, matrices, and trails in the sibling books. ∎
