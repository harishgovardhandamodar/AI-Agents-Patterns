## Chapter 15: Egress deny-by-default at fleet scale

At fleet scale the destination inventory is the security posture in readable form: enumerate allowed destinations per binary per sandbox and the outward reach of the whole fleet is on one page. New destinations are the change worth human review, and the prover flags credentialed reach automatically.

Test the fence independently of the rules: with workload networking off or fenced to the supervisor service, an empty policy should yield silence. Publish the inventory where the team that owns the risk can read it, expire unreviewed destinations on a cadence, and never let the list grow without owners.

**Definition 1 (Fleet destination inventory).** The *destination inventory* is the complete mapping $\{(\mathrm{sandbox}, \mathrm{binary}) \to \{\mathrm{destinations}\}\}$ across the fleet — every allowed (binary, destination) pair, per sandbox, reviewable on one page with per-pair owners and last-review timestamps.

**Theorem 1 (Inventory-complete posture).** If the inventory is complete and current, then the fleet's entire outward reach is known — and any connection to an unlisted destination is, by construction, either a policy violation in progress or a fence failure, with nothing in between.

*Proof.* Deny-by-default plus the outer fence means the only packets that leave are those the inventory lists (per-binary rules through the mediated channel). A connection to a listed destination is authorized by definition — reviewable, owned, prover-checked for credentialed reach. A connection to an unlisted destination contradicts the fence: either a rule was added without inventory update (process failure, visible on the next inventory diff) or the fence itself failed (mechanism failure, visible in testing with an empty policy yielding non-silence). The dichotomy is exhaustive because the fence admits no third path — there is no direct egress to misconfigure, only the mediated channel. Publish the inventory where the risk owners read it; expire the oldest unreviewed destination on sight; test the fence with an empty policy and demand silence. ∎
