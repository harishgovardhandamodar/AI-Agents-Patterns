**Definition 1 (Pinned-dataset synthesis).** Research synthesis satisfies *pinning* iff (i) every dataset version is pinned (v7, not latest), (ii) every claim traces to a logged source, (iii) synthesis sandboxes cannot reach publication channels, and (iv) one synthesis output per cycle is traced fully to sources.

**Theorem 1 (Compounding stopped at the source).** Under pinning with citation tracing, fabrication and self-distillation collapse are caught at write time or at worst at the next pin: poisoned beliefs cannot compound through downstream papers because each downstream paper re-pins rather than inheriting.

*Proof.* Fabrication (14 phantom papers) meets retrieval-against-logged-sources: unresolvable citations are flagged at write time, and synthesis sandboxes cannot publish around the check (iii). Self-distillation collapse (training successors on own outputs) meets version pinning: the successor trains on v7, not on the predecessor's outputs — capability cannot drain geometrically when each generation restarts from the pinned base. Peer-review gaming and dataset drift (the unauthored neighbors) face the same two screens. Version one dataset the synthesists use; verify citations resolve to logged sources; rehearse one unpracticed research shape. Beliefs compound, so provenance compounds with them — or the lab manufactures confidence at machine scale. ∎
