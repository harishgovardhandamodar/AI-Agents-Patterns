## Semantic Memory / Knowledge Graph Pattern Overview

Facts stored as typed entities/relations in a graph; agent queries with structured lookups instead of fuzzy recall.

**Semantic Memory / Knowledge Graph** is catalogued in the **Memory & Knowledge** part of the catalog (id `semantic-memory-kg`). It is tagged for enterprise, research, legal and is assembled from 3 components.

Extract entities and relations from interactions; store them with provenance edges; query via graph traversal plus embedding hybrid. Structured lookup beats fuzzy recall for precision — and creates the canon problem: a hallucinated relation, once stored, is queried confidently downstream, and every later traversal inherits the error as settled fact. Confidence plus provenance on every edge is what keeps the graph a memory rather than a mythology.

**Definition 1 (Gated graph edge).** An edge $e$ is *actionable* iff (i) it carries a confidence score and a provenance chain resolving to its source, and (ii) low-confidence edges are hidden from auto-actions (visible for review, never executed on) — with periodic human-verified consolidation of the graph.

**Theorem 1 (Canon requires review).** If every edge needs confidence plus provenance to be actionable, and consolidation is human-verified, then a hallucinated relation can enter the store but cannot become canon: it is either low-confidence (hidden from actions) or provenance-free (flagged at review).

*Proof.* Hallucination writes the wrong edge; gating decides whether it matters. High-confidence-with-provenance edges drive auto-actions legitimately. Low-confidence edges are hidden from auto-actions by (ii) — the wrong edge exists but cannot act. Provenance-free edges fail review (i, consolidation) — the wrong edge is visible as unsourced and removed or corrected. Canonization requires passing both gates: confident *and* provenanced. A hallucination passing both means the verifier confirmed a falsehood against its sources — possible, but now a verification failure with an audit trail, not a silent inheritance. Verify edge provenance resolves; check consolidation linkages; confirm uncertain edges cannot drive actions without review. ∎
