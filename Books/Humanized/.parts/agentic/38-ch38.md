## Memory Consolidation (Reflection-Driven) Pattern Overview

Background process distills raw interaction logs into durable, ranked memories (like sleep consolidation).

**Memory Consolidation (Reflection-Driven)** is catalogued in the **Memory & Knowledge** part of the catalog (id `memory-consolidation`). It is tagged for general, support and is assembled from 3 components.

A periodic job reviews recent episodes, extracts candidate memories, dedupes against existing entries, ranks them, attaches confidence, and stores the survivors. Like sleep, it converts the day's noise into durable structure — and like sleep, it can consolidate garbage: a one-off edge case memorized as policy, a planted instruction promoted into a durable rule. The confidence threshold plus the instruction filter are what separate consolidation from contamination.

**Definition 1 (Reviewed consolidation).** Consolidation is *reviewed* iff (i) candidates below the confidence threshold require human review before promotion (quarantined, never auto-stored), (ii) anything resembling instructions to bypass policy is never consolidated regardless of confidence, and (iii) candidates dedupe against the existing store (no duplicate entrenchment).

**Theorem 1 (Garbage stays out).** Under reviewed consolidation, one-off edge cases and planted instructions cannot become durable policy: low-confidence candidates wait for humans, instruction-like candidates are refused categorically, and duplicates merge rather than multiply.

*Proof.* The edge case (one-off, low support) scores below threshold and quarantines for human review instead of entering the store — garbage consolidation requires auto-promotion, which the threshold forbids. The plant (attacker content shaped as guidance) matches the instruction filter and is refused regardless of confidence score — the standing-instruction and self-distillation shapes from the catalog die at this filter. Dedup prevents entrenchment-by-repetition (the same bad memory re-extracted each cycle would otherwise accumulate false corroboration). Verify dedupe against the long-term store; review the reflexion links; confirm low-confidence candidates stay quarantined rather than promoted. The successor trained on its own outputs collapses precisely where consolidation lacked these three checks. ∎
