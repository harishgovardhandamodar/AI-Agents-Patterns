## Working Memory (Context Window) Pattern Overview

Treat the context window as working memory: system policy, tools, retrieved facts, and recent turns — with a strict priority order.

**Working Memory (Context Window)** is catalogued in the **Memory & Knowledge** part of the catalog (id `working-memory-context`). It is tagged for general and is assembled from 2 components.

Layered context — policy > task > retrieved > history — with truncation from the bottom: history first, policy never. Critical instructions pinned; attention budgeted per turn; behavior verified at context extremes. The catastrophic failure is CWE-style instruction loss: truncation drops the safety policy block, and guardrails vanish mid-task — the agent keeps acting with the controls silently gone.

**Definition 1 (Policy-pinned context).** A context assembly is *policy-pinned* iff (i) the system safety policy occupies a pinned block re-asserted every $N$ turns, (ii) truncation removes history before ever touching policy, task, or retrieved constraints, and (iii) an attention budget caps history tokens per turn.

**Theorem 1 (Guardrails survive context pressure).** Under policy-pinned assembly with attention budgets and needle-test verification, no amount of history growth removes the safety policy — dilution degrades recall, never protection.

*Proof.* Truncation order is fixed: history first, policy never (ii) — the CWE-style loss requires truncating the policy block, which the order forbids by construction. Re-assertion every $N$ turns (i) repairs even adversarial or accidental policy displacement: the block returns on schedule regardless of what intervened. The attention budget (iii) bounds dilution: capping history tokens per turn keeps the signal-to-noise ratio above the level where instruction adherence collapses. Needle tests at context extremes verify the property empirically rather than assuming it — context-compression alone will not save it, because compression summarizes content while pinning protects position. Verify truncation never touches policy; test behavior at extremes; re-assert on schedule. ∎
