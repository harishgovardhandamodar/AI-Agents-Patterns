## Interrupt & Resume (Checkpointed HITL) Pattern Overview

Agent serializes its state, requests permission, and can be resumed days later by a different operator without redoing work.

**Interrupt & Resume (Checkpointed HITL)** is catalogued in the **Agent Loop & Control** part of the catalog (id `interrupt-resume`). It is tagged for finance, devops, procurement and is assembled from 3 components.

Checkpoint state (plan, args, evidence) at approval points; persist; notify the approver; resume with idempotency keys so side effects never double-execute. Days-later resumption by a different operator is the point: the checkpoint carries everything the new approver needs, and idempotency carries the guarantee that resumed work does not redo what already happened. Two time-bombs ship with the pattern: the world changing between proposal and approval (TOCTOU staleness), and the approval itself aging past relevance.

**Definition 1 (Fresh-idempotent resume).** A resume is *fresh-idempotent* iff (i) preconditions are re-validated at execution time (not approval time), (ii) every side effect carries an idempotency key, and (iii) approvals carry expiry windows — a stale approval cannot authorize, and a repeated execution cannot double-apply.

**Theorem 1 (Time cannot break the resume).** Under fresh-idempotent resume, neither waiting (days between checkpoint and approval) nor repetition (resume re-running steps) changes the outcome: preconditions are checked against the world as it is, keys dedupe what already happened, expiry bounds how old an approval may be.

*Proof.* Waiting: the world changes between proposal and approval (TOCTOU), so approval-time validation is stale by execution — re-validation at execution time checks the current world, and expiry windows bound how old the approval may be before it dies entirely. Repetition: resume re-running a completed side effect meets its idempotency key and returns the cached result instead of re-executing (duplicate execution impossible by construction). Together: waiting is neutralized by fresh checks plus expiry, repetition by keys. Verify keys exist before resume; verify preconditions and expiry at resume time — a checkpoint without keys is a duplicate waiting to happen, an approval without expiry is a stale permission waiting to be abused. ∎
