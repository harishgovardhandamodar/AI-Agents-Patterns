## Context Compression / Summarization Pattern Overview

Summarize old turns/tool outputs to fit context while preserving decisions, open items, and constraints.

**Context Compression / Summarization** is catalogued in the **Memory & Knowledge** part of the catalog (id `context-compression`). It is tagged for general and is assembled from 2 components.

Rolling summarizer over history; pin decisions, open items, and constraints; drop raw tool output after summarization. Compression trades tokens for risk on two fronts simultaneously: lossy compression drops constraints ('Do not exceed \$5000' doesn't survive summarization), and faithful compression preserves attacker text into 'instructions' that outlive the original (summary injection). The pin-list plus untrusted-data marking plus before/after audit is the three-part answer.

**Definition 1 (Lossless-authority compression).** Compression is *lossless-authority* iff (i) the constraint pin-list (decisions, open items, limits, identities) is copied verbatim, never summarized, (ii) summaries are marked as untrusted derived data, and (iii) decisions are audited before vs. after compression for divergence.

**Theorem 1 (Authority survives compression iff pinned).** Under lossless-authority compression, no constraint, decision, or unresolved item is altered by summarization — while everything else may be lossy, because nothing else governs.

*Proof.* The pin-list bypasses the summarizer entirely (i): verbatim copy preserves limits, decisions, and identities bit-for-bit, so 'Do not exceed \$5000' survives no matter what the summary does to the surrounding prose. Untrusted marking (ii) prevents promotion: summarized attacker text arrives labeled derived-and-untrusted, so it cannot graduate into instructions — injection preserved is still marked hostile. The before/after audit (iii) catches what the first two missed: comparing decisions across compression detects drift the pin-list didn't cover. Compression without pinning rewrites authority (the catalogued failures); compression with pinning rewrites only narrative. Verify pinned constraints survive; treat summaries as suspect until checked; confirm injected content cannot graduate into instructions through the summary. ∎
